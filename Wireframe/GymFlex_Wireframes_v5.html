How to check in
Every visit you log counts toward your monthly total and sets your price next month.
1
Arrive at the gym
Be physically inside or at the entrance. GPS confirms you're within 50m — not from home, not from the street outside.
2
Tap "Check in now"
The app checks your GPS automatically. If you're in range, the QR scanner opens.
3
Scan the tablet at reception
The gym has a tablet showing a QR code. Point your phone at it. The code changes every hour so it can't be faked.
4
Done — visit logged
Your monthly visit count updates instantly. You'll see your progress toward the next tier.
🔒
Privacy: We only check your location when you tap check in. We never track you continuously or store your location history.
